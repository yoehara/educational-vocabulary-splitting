# Educational Vocabulary Splitting

Code and aggregate results for the paper

> Yo Ehara. **Educational Vocabulary Splitting: Partitioning Corpora to Control the Influx of Unseen Words.** In *Findings of the Association for Computational Linguistics: EACL 2027*.

The code, configuration files, document identifiers, checksums, and aggregate results for this paper are being prepared and will be uploaded to this repository.

No corpus text, trained checkpoint, or corpus-trained tokenizer will be distributed. The BNC and BCCWJ are licensed corpora and must be obtained from their distributors.
